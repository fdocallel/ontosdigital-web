Civil engineer · automation and AI · founder of ONTOS

I am a civil engineer, and my work is putting information in order with code and artificial intelligence. I come from the projects where you learn that the hard way: six years leading models, data and automation on major international works, where a misplaced piece of data costs millions.

Today I work from Segovia, for Spain and remotely, on two fronts that feed each other. I build ONTOS, a personal and business digital twin kept up to date by an AI, and I do information engineering on commission: automation, data and BIM.

Where I come from

I spent the six years before ONTOS on projects where the volume of information outgrows any one person, and the only way through is building systems that keep it in order on their own.

  • Sydney Metro West (Australia)Modelling and automation on the Sydney metro works, with 41 cross passages delivered through code-generated models.
  • HS2 (United Kingdom)Models, data and information coordination on the new British high-speed line.
  • Northern Water (Adelaide, Australia)Water infrastructure of 140 ML per day, with data as the backbone of the project.
  • NEOM (Saudi Arabia)Automation and information management on one of the largest infrastructure developments in the world.

That work earned an R&D award for automation and AI, and part of it is published.
