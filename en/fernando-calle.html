Fernando Calle

Civil engineer · automation and AI · founder of ONTOS

I am a civil engineer, and my work is putting information in order with code and artificial intelligence. I come from the projects where you learn that the hard way: six years leading models, data and automation on major international works, where the order of the information sets the deadlines and the costs.

Today I work on two fronts that feed each other. I build ONTOS, the system that puts my own life in order, and I bring the same method to people and companies, from their context for AI to a website or a world in 3D.

Where I come from
