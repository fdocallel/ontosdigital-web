How it works

Three steps, and the phone does the heavy lifting.

  1. One photo per garment

    It crops itself, type and colour are suggested, and a record is kept with brand, size and fabric if they can be read on the label.

  2. The wardrobe sorts itself

    Hangers, cards or table. Shelves by colour, type, brand or by what suits you best. Filters and search on top.

  3. Outfits and verdicts

    With your skin tone and your silhouette, each garment gets a colour and cut verdict, and outfits come out that you can see on yourself.

What it is and what it isn't

A prototype in use

It's the app I use every day with my own clothes. The first public version will be for iPhone.

Nothing leaves the phone

Photos and records live on your device. Export and import are up to you, with no account and no server.

The try-ons are AI

The outfits shown on me are simulations for comparison. Exact size and drape are checked when you get dressed.

The stylist is still human

The colour and cut verdicts come from an analysis made by a professional. The app applies it to each garment; it doesn't replace it.
