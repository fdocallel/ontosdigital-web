Applications · 3D modelling from a drone

From a drone flight to a house in 3D.

A 26-second orbit with the drone. From that video, and without the images ever leaving the computer, comes the exterior of the house in 3D: the textured scan and a simple geometry you can work with.

Watch the videoBack to Applications

The process and the result, in 34 seconds

It is a house built from shipping containers. The video shows the flight, the intermediate steps and the viewer with both versions of the model.

How it is done

Five steps, all on a home computer, with open-source tools.

  1. Frames come out of the video

    One frame every 0.2 seconds: 131 images of the house from every angle of the orbit.

  2. Every camera goes back to its place

    The software matches the images against each other, places the 131 cameras back in space and builds a point cloud.

  3. From the cloud, a textured mesh

    Eight million points become a continuous surface with the real photographs on top.

  4. The boundary is drawn

    By hand, on the model: what matters stays and the rest is discarded.

  5. A simple geometry

    Sections at several heights give the real outline. From there come the volumes and the roof, free of noise: the version you can compare with a plan, use to think about an extension or to count containers.

What it is and what it is not

Exterior, not interior

Only what the camera sees from outside gets modelled. Porches and shaded areas come out worse.

No construction-grade scale

This model is not calibrated with field measurements. To measure, you need two or three passes at different heights and a tape measure.

Nothing leaves home

The images are processed locally with OpenDroneMap. Photos of a home do not travel to someone else's server.
