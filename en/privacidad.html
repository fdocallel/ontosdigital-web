What data is processed and what for

  • Contact emails. If you write to the contact address, the data you include is processed (your email address and the content of the message) for the sole purpose of answering your query and, if you ask for one, preparing a proposal. Legal basis: pre-contractual measures taken at your request and legitimate interest in attending to the communication (art. 6.1.b and 6.1.f GDPR). Messages are kept for as long as the conversation lasts and, if it leads to a professional relationship, for the duration of that relationship and the associated legal periods.
  • Audience measurement. This site uses GoatCounter, a visit counter that uses no cookies and does not identify the visitor; it only produces aggregate counts (page views, approximate origin). That is why this site shows no cookie banner: it does not use them.
  • Contact form. If you use the contact form, the data you provide is processed: name, organisation, email and message. That data is transmitted through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) for the sole purpose of delivering the message to the ONTOS inbox; it is not used for any other purpose, it is not passed on to third parties and it is kept only in the mailbox. Legal basis: consent of the data subject when submitting the form (art. 6.1.a GDPR).

What is not done

Data is not passed on to third parties except where legally required, no profiling is carried out and no advertising is sent. Your email address is used exclusively to reply to you. The typeface on this site is self-hosted: it does not connect to third-party servers to load fonts.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to hola@ontosdigital.es. If you believe that any processing is not correct, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
