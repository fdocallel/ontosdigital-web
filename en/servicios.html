How a project starts

We start with what you want someone to be able to do or feel. From there, we agree on what to build.

  1. The idea and its purpose

    We define the audience, content, interactions and devices it needs to work on.

  2. A first version

    We try out the visual direction and interaction with something concrete for you to review.

  3. The agreed delivery

    Files, instructions and checks. Scope, budget, timeline and maintenance are agreed for each project.
