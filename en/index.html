What ONTOS is

Ontos, from the Greek ὄντος: “that which is”. Almost nobody has an information problem: what they have is an order problem. Data lives in folders, admin lives in your head, knowledge lives in each person's experience, and putting the whole picture together costs more than the decision you had to make.

ONTOS is the digital twin of that: an ordered model of what you have, what you do and what you know, with artificial intelligence working on top of it every day.

◉

The data, in one place

Every fact lives once. Views, reports and summaries are generated from it, so nothing contradicts itself or goes stale in a forgotten copy.

✦

The AI that operates it

You tell it in one sentence what has happened and it updates everything that is affected. You ask in plain language and it answers with your own data, citing where each thing comes from.

✓

The audit that keeps watch

A system nobody watches degrades. An automatic check runs every day to make sure nothing is lost, that the views match their source and that no loose end is left hanging.

Everything runs on your machine. No compulsory cloud, no account to create, none of your data feeding anybody's product.

Where it stands today

ONTOS is not a promise: it is what I use every day since June 2026. What you see in the cases is running on my own instance, with my real data.

It is in private pilot, and I open it up one person at a time: I sit down with each of them on day one, we build their model and we switch on only the modules that are useful to them. You learn and you fix before taking the next step.

There is no public price yet. I would rather learn with the first few people before putting a figure on it, and tell you so straight instead of selling you a list of features.
