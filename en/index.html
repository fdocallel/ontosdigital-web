What ontos is

ontos is a personal assistant supported by a living model of your reality. Its foundation is the digital twin: a partial representation of what you know, have, do and want to do, kept up to date. The assistant uses that context with artificial intelligence to understand your situation, help you decide and act according to your judgement.

◉

The data, in one place

Every fact lives once. Views, reports and summaries are generated from it, so nothing contradicts itself or goes stale in a forgotten copy.

✦

The AI that operates it

You tell it in one sentence what has happened and it updates everything that is affected. You ask in plain language and it answers with your own data, citing where each thing comes from.

✓

The audit that keeps watch

A system nobody watches degrades. An automatic check runs every day to make sure nothing is lost, that the views match their source and that no loose end is left hanging.

During the pilot, your model lives on a computer owned by Fernando, in a separate space. AI engines may run remotely; what leaves that machine, and when, is explained in the pilot conditions.

Where it stands today

ontos is not a promise: it is what I have used every day since June 2026. What you see in the cases is running on my own instance, with my real data.

It is in a private pilot, and I open it up one person at a time: I sit down with each of them on day one, we build their model and we switch on only the modules that are useful to them. We learn and fix things before taking the next step.

There is no public price yet. I would rather learn with the first few people before putting a figure on it, and tell you so straight instead of selling you a list of features.
